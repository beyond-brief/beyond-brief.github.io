We connect business thinking with the ability to build.

Beyond Brief is a strategy, customer experience and digital consulting studio. We bring research, strategy, customer experience, creative thinking and technology together, because the best work happens when they aren't separate conversations.

Most briefs describe a symptom. A campaign that needs to perform. A process that's too slow. A product that should exist. Our job is to find the cause, and then build the thing that fixes it.

— How we work

The shape of a project.

Timelines vary, but most engagements move through four phases, with a working version on the table by the midpoint.

  1. Week 1–2

    Understand

    We start with the business, not the brief. Interviews, data, the process as it really runs.

  2. Week 2–3

    Decide

    We agree what we're solving, what success looks like and what we're deliberately leaving out.

  3. Week 3–8

    Make

    A working version early, on realistic data, so feedback is about something real.

  4. Week 8+

    Prove

    We measure the result against what we agreed, hand over properly and say plainly what we'd do next.

— Principles

What we don't compromise on.

We show, then tell.

A rough prototype on day ten beats a polished strategy deck on day sixty.

We write down the assumptions.

Every recommendation comes with the evidence behind it and its limits.

People stay in charge.

Technology, including AI, supports the work. Anything customer-facing is reviewed by a person, and every system suggestion is logged.

We keep scope honest.

What we won't do is as clear as what we will.

What we are.

  • A small, senior team that stays with the work
  • Researchers who can design, and designers who can build
  • Honest about scope, cost and risk
  • Comfortable with complexity and regulation

What we aren't.

  • A slide factory
  • A body-shop selling hours
  • Hype merchants for the latest tool
  • An agency that says yes to everything
