02 Education & EdTech

Better systems for better learning experiences.

Education involves far more than the classroom. Behind every student journey are admissions, communication, scheduling, payments, support and countless daily decisions. We help institutions connect these experiences through thoughtful digital products and better processes.

— What we can help build

Systems for the whole student journey.

  1. Student Management Platforms

    Centralised student profiles, enrolment information, attendance, academic administration and communication history.

  2. Admissions & Enrollment CRM

    Lead capture, applicant tracking, automated follow-ups, document workflows and enrolment funnel analytics.

  3. Student & Parent Portals

    Secure access to schedules, attendance, assignments, updates, invoices and important communications.

  4. Learning Experience Platforms

    Course discovery, learning content access, progress visualisation, student engagement and feedback tools.

  5. Communication & Campaign Management

    Enrolment campaigns, open-day registration, student onboarding, reminders and lifecycle communication.

  6. Education Analytics

    Admissions conversion, engagement, retention, capacity planning and operational reporting.

  7. Operational Automation

    Staff workflows, approvals, scheduling, information routing and repetitive administrative tasks.

— The challenges

What gets in the way.

01

Enquiries go cold between open day and application.

Follow-up depends on who remembers to send it.

02

Families chase information across five channels.

Timetables, invoices and updates live in different places.

03

Staff re-enter the same data again and again.

Admissions, finance and academic teams each keep their own records.

Less administration. More room to focus on students.

Disconnected systems create unnecessary work for staff and confusing experiences for students and families. A more connected approach can make everyday interactions clearer, faster and easier to manage.
