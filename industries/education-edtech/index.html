05 Education & EdTech

Better systems for better learning experiences.

We design learner journeys, learning experience platforms and the digital services around them, so students can find the right course, keep their pace and finish what they start.

Discuss a project See it working
— What we understand

The challenges we hear.

01

Learners lose pace after the first weeks.

Without visible progress and a way to catch up, a busy week becomes a reason to stop.

02

Enquiries go cold between open day and application.

Follow-up depends on who remembers to send it.

03

Families and students chase information across channels.

Timetables, invoices and updates live in different places.

— How we help

Where our practices fit.

Customer Experience, UX & Digital ExperiencesLearner journeys, learning experience platforms, student portals and digital learning interfaces, researched and tested with learners.Customer Growth, Loyalty & GamificationProgress, milestones and gentle gamification that keep learners going, plus re-engagement for those who stall.Campaigns, Data & CreativeEnrolment and open-day campaigns built on applicant data, from first enquiry to first day.Strategy & Market ResearchResearch into learner needs and demand before a new programme or platform.

Good to know. Education systems handle student and often children's data. We design for access controls, consent, data minimisation and the data protection rules that apply to each institution.
