01 Strategy & Market Research

Better decisions start with better questions.

Before anyone designs or builds, it's worth knowing which problem is worth solving. We find out what your market wants, where competitors are weak and where your best opportunity really is.

Talk to us about this
Four questions, one decision
— Sound familiar?

The problems we hear most.

01

You're about to commit budget to a new market, product or audience.

And the case for it rests on a few slides and a lot of optimism.

02

Your positioning sounds like everyone else's.

Customers can't tell you apart, so they compare you on price.

03

Research gets done, then sits in a folder.

Reports describe the market but stop short of telling you what to do next.

— Our approach

How we'd tackle it.

  1. Frame the decision

    We agree the decision the research must support, and what evidence would change your mind.

  2. Listen widely

    Customer interviews, sales conversations, search behaviour and competitor offers, side by side.

  3. Size what matters

    Simple, transparent models you can adjust yourself, with every assumption written down.

  4. Recommend

    A clear point of view, the risks attached and the first three moves.

— In practice

Know the market before making the move.

Research is only useful when it changes a decision. Every study starts with the decision it needs to support.
