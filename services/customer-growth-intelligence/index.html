— Gamification, done properly

A mechanic starts with a behaviour, not a badge.

Every mechanic we design links one customer behaviour to one business goal, with a reward that's worth earning and a way to prove it worked.

  1. Business goalTurn registered rewards members into active card users.
  2. Target behaviourThree card payments in a calendar month.
  3. MechanicA monthly challenge with visible progress, shown where the customer already looks.
  4. RewardOne tier step and bonus points, earned once and capped per customer.
  5. ProofCompared against a holdout group, so the effect on behaviour is measured, not assumed.
