04 Customer Growth & Loyalty

Give customers a reason to come back.

Winning a customer is expensive. Keeping one is mostly a matter of noticing the right moments. We help you see where customers grow, stall or drift away, and what to do at each point.

The problems we hear most.

01

You find out about churn when it's already happened.

By the time the report lands, the customer has gone.

02

Every channel holds a piece of the customer.

Nobody sees the whole relationship in one place.

03

Loyalty means discounts.

Which trains customers to wait for the next one.

— In practice

Growth happens between the first interaction and the next one.

The moments that matter look different in every sector: a card that's never used, a basket abandoned, a member who stops booking.

— Our approach

How we'd work with you.

Every engagement is shaped to the problem, but this is the rhythm we usually follow.

  1. Define the lifecycle

    Agree what onboarding, active, loyal and at risk actually mean for your business.

  2. Find the moments

    Map the points where customers decide to stay, grow or leave.

  3. Design the response

    The right message, offer or human contact for each moment.

  4. Test against a control

    Prove which actions change behaviour, then scale them.
