Each stage records what the customer is doing, where, and how it feels, so the moment worth redesigning stands out. Select a stage to see it.

Journey map · a working learner's first month on a learning platformIllustrative
Doing
Finds a short course through a colleague's post.
Touchpoints
Social post · Course page
Feeling
Curious
Pain point
Course page doesn't say how long a lesson takes.
Opportunity
Show weekly time needed up front.
Doing
Signs up on a phone during a lunch break.
Touchpoints
Sign-up form · Welcome email
Feeling
Motivated
Pain point
Seven form fields before the first lesson.
Opportunity
Start learning first, finish the profile later.
Doing
Completes lesson one on the bus home.
Touchpoints
Mobile app · Lesson player
Feeling
Confident
Pain point
Progress resets when the app is closed mid-lesson.
Opportunity
Save progress automatically, every minute.
Doing
Misses two sessions after a busy week at work.
Touchpoints
Generic reminder emails
Feeling
Losing pace
Pain point
Can't see how far behind they are, or how to catch up.
Opportunity
A weekly goal and a short catch-up path.
Doing
Returns once, then decides whether to continue.
Touchpoints
Progress page · Certificate
Feeling
Undecided
Pain point
Nothing marks how much they've already achieved.
Opportunity
Milestones and a visible finish line.
Persona · illustrative

The working learner

Studies after work, mostly on a phone, in sessions of fifteen minutes or less.

Needs
Short lessons, visible progress, a plan that survives a busy week.
Frustrated by
Losing their place, generic reminders, not knowing how far is left.
Success looks like
Finishing the course without having to start over.
