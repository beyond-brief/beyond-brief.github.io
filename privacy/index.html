Privacy policy.

What this website collects, why, and how to change your choice. In short: analytics only runs if you accept it, and what you type into the contact form is used only to reply to you.

Who we are

This website is run by BEYOND BRIEF. For any privacy question or request, use the contact form on this site.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only.

Analytics, only with your consent

We use Google Analytics 4, a service from Google, to understand how the site is used. It runs only if you choose “Accept analytics” in the banner. If you decline, or don't answer, Google Analytics isn't loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

When you accept, Google Analytics records:
  • the pages you view and how long you stay;
  • how you arrived, for example from a search engine, LinkedIn, another website or directly;
  • your approximate location (country and city), derived from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 doesn't store IP addresses;
  • your device type, browser and screen size;
  • which menu links, service and industry links and contact links you click, and how you use the Customer 360 demo, for example which scenario or tab you open.
We don't send Google your name, email address, anything you type into the contact form, or any identifier that tells us who you are. Google's advertising features (Google signals and ad personalisation) are switched off.

Cookies

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_<ID>, which distinguish visits. They last up to two years unless you delete them or withdraw consent.

We also save your choice (accepted or declined) in your browser's local storage under bb-consent, so we don't ask on every page. It stays until you clear your browser's site data.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. If you decline after accepting, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser and stop loading Google Analytics. You can also block cookies in your browser settings or use Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How long analytics data is kept

Google keeps the analytics event data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, and then deletes it. We only look at that data in aggregate, for example as visitor counts and page popularity.

The contact form

When you send the form, what you entered goes directly from your browser to Netlify's form service, which stores it and checks it for spam. Netlify also records your IP address, browser details and the page you sent it from. We read your message there and may receive a copy by email. We use it only to reply to your enquiry, we don't add you to mailing lists, and it's never sent to Google Analytics. We keep it only as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry, then delete it. Netlify may process this information outside your country. To have your details corrected or deleted, use the contact form.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, and may log it for security. The site's typeface is loaded from Fontshare, whose servers also receive your IP address when the font loads. See GitHub's privacy statement for how GitHub handles this information.

Google's role

Google processes analytics data on our behalf. Information may be processed on Google's servers outside your country. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the analytics we use don't identify you, we usually can't link analytics data to a specific person. You can always withdraw consent as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect data, we'll update this page and the date below.
