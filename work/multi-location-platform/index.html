01.

Intended user

Owners, branch managers and front-desk teams of appointment-based businesses with several locations.

02.

Business problem

Each branch keeps its own diary and customer list. Loyalty, reminders and reporting are manual or missing.

03.

Core workflow

A customer books online at any branch. Their profile, visits and points follow them, reminders go out automatically, and managers compare branches in one place.

— Features

What the concept covers.

  1. Customer-facing booking with location, service and time
  2. Today's appointments across branches
  3. Shared customer profiles and visit history
  4. Loyalty tiers and rules
  5. Branch utilisation reporting
  6. Automated reminders and win-back journeys
— Expected benefit

The kind of improvement it targets.

  • Fewer no-shows through reminders
  • A consistent customer experience at every location
  • Spare capacity made visible
  • Less time switching between tools

Independent Concept Demonstration. Designed and built by us on synthetic data. It isn't a client project, doesn't reproduce any organisation's systems and reports no real performance.
