— What's real

What this is, and what it isn't.

What it is

A capability demonstration

Customer Intelligence OS shows how Beyond Brief approaches customer intelligence, personalisation and campaign management. It isn't a software product for sale, and we don't report client results for it.

On this page

An interactive demonstration

Three synthetic customers and a transparent rules engine, running in your browser. No customer data, no connected systems and no messages sent.

How we apply it

A solution shaped around your business

We provide the consulting, experience design and implementation support to build this kind of workflow around a business's own customer data, offers, consent rules and approval steps, usually inside the platforms it already uses. Each implementation needs its own data, security and legal review.
