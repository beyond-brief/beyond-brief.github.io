— How the decision works

Twelve steps, every one visible.

The engine runs the same sequence for every profile. Change a consent setting, the preferred channel or the time of day and it starts again from step one.

  1. 01 Load the selected customer profile
  2. 02 Evaluate interest signals
  3. 03 Identify matching segments
  4. 04 Check offer eligibility
  5. 05 Exclude ineligible offers
  6. 06 Rank eligible offers with transparent rules
  7. 07 Select the highest-ranked offer
  8. 08 Evaluate marketing consent
  9. 09 Choose the preferred permitted channel
  10. 10 Apply frequency and quiet-hours rules
  11. 11 Generate the matching preview
  12. 12 Explain the decision

Consent comes before preference. A preferred channel is only used when marketing permission for it is active. If no channel is permitted, the right decision is not to communicate, however strong the customer's interest.

— Real and simulated

What this demonstration actually does.

This is the Rules-Based Next Best Offer Demonstration. It is not a trained AI recommendation engine.

Real, working logic

  • Deterministic decision engine evaluating 6 segment rules and 4 offers on every change
  • Eligibility, exclusion and ranking computed live, with the score breakdown shown
  • Consent always checked before preference; suppression can't be overridden from the controls
  • Frequency cap, quiet hours and global unsubscribe rules
  • Email, SMS and push previews built from profile and offer data
  • Explanations generated from the evaluation trace, not written per customer
  • 16 automated business-rule tests run separately from the interface

Simulated or illustrative

  • Customers, transactions, browsing and consent records are synthetic
  • Portraits are licensed stock photos of models, not the customers
  • No message is ever sent; no messaging, CRM or banking system is connected
  • Offers, terms and partner names are illustrative placeholders
  • Interest signals are fixed demo values, not computed from real activity
  • The link to Campaign Intelligence is conceptual; there's no shared backend
  • AI-powered ranking is a possible future capability, not part of this version

About the portraits. Real photographs of models, licensed from Freepik. They're illustrative only: the people pictured aren't customers, didn't provide this data and don't endorse anything shown.
