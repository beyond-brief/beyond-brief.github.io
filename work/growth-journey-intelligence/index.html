01.

The problem

Teams can describe their customer journey on a whiteboard, but they can't see it in the data. Drop-off is noticed months later, in a quarterly report.

02.

The idea

A live view of the journey that shows where each segment slows down, what usually happens next and which action is worth trying.

03.

What we explored

Comparing segments on the same journey, turning drop-off points into plain-language insights and pairing each insight with a testable action.

Independent Concept Demonstration. Designed and built by us on synthetic data. It isn't a client project, doesn't reproduce any organisation's systems and reports no real performance.
