A fictional school group with synthetic applicants and students. No real student data.Use the tabs to move through the views
01.

Intended user

Admissions officers, school administrators and heads of year who need one view of applicants and students.

02.

Business problem

Enquiries, applications, records and family communication are spread across spreadsheets, inboxes and separate systems.

03.

Core workflow

An enquiry becomes an application, an offer and an enrolment. The record follows the student, and families receive the right message at each step.

— Features

What the concept covers.

  1. Applicant pipeline with stage owners
  2. Enrolment funnel with drop-off view
  3. Student records with guardian contact preferences
  4. Communication calendar
  5. Attendance overview by year group
  6. Term dashboard
— Expected benefit

The kind of improvement it targets.

  • Fewer enquiries lost between stages
  • Less double entry across teams
  • Clearer, more consistent family communication
  • Earlier visibility of attendance issues

Independent Concept Demonstration. Designed and built by us on synthetic data. It isn't a client project, doesn't reproduce any organisation's systems and reports no real performance.
