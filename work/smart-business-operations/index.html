04 Smart Business Operations

A clearer view of the business.

Customers, schedules, tasks and performance in one management view.

Type
Concept prototype
Area

Scope

Concept 04 · week boardOperations hub, week 43Synthetic data
A fictional service business. Names and figures are invented.Switch views, move tasks, resolve exceptions
01.

The problem

Managers rebuild the same status picture every week from customer lists, calendars, task boards and spreadsheets that don't agree with each other.

02.

The idea

One view where customers, bookings, tasks and performance sit together, and exceptions rise to the top on their own.

03.

What we explored

How a single screen can switch between a pipeline, a weekly schedule and a task board without losing context, and which numbers a manager really needs each morning.

Independent Concept Demonstration. Designed and built by us on synthetic data. It isn't a client project, doesn't reproduce any organisation's systems and reports no real performance.
